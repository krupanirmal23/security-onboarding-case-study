North Star
Activated accounts
New orgs with ≥1 enrolled device and ≥1 policy enforced on its traffic within [N] days of signup. N: [Team to decide]
Input

Time to first policy

Signup → first policy enforced on an enrolled device. Median per weekly cohort.

Checklist completion

Share of new orgs finishing each checklist step. Shows where they stop.
Input

Setup support tickets

Tickets and forum posts tagged setup, per 100 new orgs. Lower is better.

Misconfiguration rate

Share of activated orgs with a policy that blocks its own admin, blocks nothing it matched, or is rolled back within [N] days. Definition: [Team to confirm]
Guardrail

Security incidents

Incidents traced to a template, a sample-data draft, or a helper-suggested change. Any rise vs control stops the rollout.
